* Introduction & Overview
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: introduction
:END:

=denote-sync= is an open-source Emacs package developed by Sam Kleinman
(=garen@tychoish.com=) that enables bidirectional synchronization between local
[[https://github.com/protesilaos/denote][Denote]] notes and external cloud services, including Google Docs.

Repository: [[https://github.com/tychoish/denote-sync][https://github.com/tychoish/denote-sync]] \\
Developer Contact: *garen@tychoish.com*

Your privacy is fundamental to the design of =denote-sync=. This Privacy Policy
explains how =denote-sync= accesses, processes, and protects your information when you
use Google Docs synchronization features.

* Architecture: Local-First & Zero Central Servers
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: architecture
:END:

=denote-sync= is designed around a strictly *local-first* architecture:

1. *Direct Client-to-API Communication*: Synchronization occurs exclusively between
   your local machine and Google's official API endpoints (=docs.googleapis.com= and
   =drive.googleapis.com=) via the local =gog= CLI helper.
2. *No Central Backend*: =denote-sync= operates without any central server, cloud proxy,
   intermediate relay, or remote database. The developer does not host, operate, or maintain
   any servers that receive or process your note contents or authentication tokens.
3. *No Third-Party Access*: Because there are no intermediate servers, neither the author
   of =denote-sync= nor any third party has access to your notes, Google credentials, or
   account metadata.

* Google OAuth Scopes Requested
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: oauth-scopes
:END:

When configuring Google Docs synchronization, =denote-sync= requests only the minimal
scopes strictly necessary to provide note synchronization:

| Scope | Category | Justification |
|-------+----------+---------------|
| =https://www.googleapis.com/auth/documents= | Sensitive | Used to create new Google Docs from local notes, retrieve document text and structure during note pull/refresh, and write updated content to Google Docs during note push. |
| =https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file= | Sensitive | Used strictly to create, locate, and organize documents within folders created or opened by =denote-sync=. It does *not* read, list, or modify other files in your Google Drive. |
| =https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email= | Non-sensitive | Used to identify the authorized Google Account email address locally so =denote-sync= can associate notes with the correct account in multi-account environments. |
| =openid= | Non-sensitive | Used to verify user authentication identity during the OAuth 2.0 handshake. |

** Scope Minimization Guarantee
=denote-sync= intentionally avoids broad Drive access (=https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive=).
By utilizing =drive.file=, the application's Drive permissions are permanently constrained
strictly to files created by =denote-sync= or files explicitly selected by you.

* Information Collected and How It Is Used
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: information-collected
:END:

** 1. Authentication Credentials and Tokens
- *What is stored*: OAuth 2.0 refresh tokens and short-lived access tokens.
- *Where it is stored*: Stored exclusively on your local computer within an encrypted
  file keyring managed by =gog= (=~/.local/share/gogcli/keyring=), protected by your
  passphrase (=GOG_KEYRING_PASSWORD=).
- *How it is used*: Used solely by local processes to authenticate requests directly to
  Google APIs. Tokens are never transmitted to any other entity.

** 2. Note Contents and Document Metadata
- *What is processed*: Markdown and Org-mode text content, document titles, identifiers,
  and revision timestamps.
- *Where it is stored*: On your local storage drive in your Denote note files, and in
  Google Docs within your personal or workspace Google Account.
- *How it is used*: Used exclusively to perform synchronization (pushing local updates to
  Google Docs and pulling remote revisions back into Emacs).

** 3. Telemetry and Analytics
- =denote-sync= contains *zero telemetry, tracking, analytics, or error-reporting beacons*.
- No background logging or behavioral data is collected, monitored, or transmitted.

* Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: data-retention-deletion
:END:

** Local Storage
All data stored by =denote-sync= resides entirely on your local filesystem:
- Denote notes: in your configured =denote-directory=.
- OAuth credentials and keys: in =~/.local/share/gogcli/= and =~/.config/gogcli/=.

** Deletion and Revocation Procedures
You maintain total control over your data and access permissions:

1. *Revoke Google OAuth Access*:
   You can revoke =denote-sync='s access to your Google account at any time via Google Account
   Security Settings:
   [[https://myaccount.google.com/permissions][https://myaccount.google.com/permissions]]
   Revocation immediately invalidates all active and stored tokens.

2. *Delete Local Tokens and Credentials*:
   To remove all locally cached tokens for an account, run:
   #+begin_src bash
   gog auth remove <your-email@example.com>
   #+end_src
   Or delete the local keyring directory entirely:
   #+begin_src bash
   rm -rf ~/.local/share/gogcli/keyring
   #+end_src

3. *Delete Synced Documents*:
   Documents created in Google Docs remain under your ownership and can be deleted directly
   from [[https://drive.google.com/][Google Drive]].

* Security
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: security
:END:

We apply industry best practices to safeguard your data:
- *In-Transit Security*: All communication with Google APIs is secured using modern TLS 1.3
  encryption.
- *At-Rest Security*: OAuth refresh tokens are encrypted at rest on your machine using
  AES-GCM encryption in the =gog= file keyring backend.
- *Minimal Privilege*: By restricting scopes to =drive.file=, the blast radius of any
  local token compromise is strictly confined to files created by the application.
